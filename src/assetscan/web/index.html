the maths

fame
Named you ÷ everyone shown the asset. Does the asset make people think of you?
uniqueness
Named you ÷ everyone who named any brand. When they think of a brand, is it only you?
score
100 × famew × uniqueness1−w, a weighted geometric mean. It is zero if either is zero, because an asset needs both.
intervals
Wilson 95% intervals on fame and uniqueness. The score range comes from 4,000 seeded draws of a Dirichlet posterior, so a thin sample shows up as a wide range.
grid
Use or lose, Invest, Avoid solo use, Test or ignore. The % shown is the probability the asset really sits in that box, given your sample.
